01 / About

Optics at the intersection of physics, engineering, and biology.

My work has centered on turning experimental needs into reliable optical capabilities. I enjoy building up optical systems, bringing together optomechanics, lasers, detectors, electronics, control, and analysis to make challenging measurements possible.

Across academic research environments, I have developed custom microscope configurations, integrated optical tweezers with advanced imaging platforms, maintained ultrafast laser systems, and supported researchers through instrument training and troubleshooting. My background combines experimental physics and chemistry with quantitative biological applications.

I am interested in optical engineering, photonics, microscopy, and scientific-instrumentation roles where practical system-building experience and cross-disciplinary problem solving can contribute to product and research development.

02 / Selected technical projects

Design, integration, and experimental problem-solving.

Examples of optical platforms and instrumentation I have helped develop, integrate, or operate. Further technical details and project photographs can be added as the portfolio evolves.

PROJECT 01

Four-channel time-resolved confocal microscope

Developed a custom time-resolved, four-channel confocal microscope for quantitative fluorescence measurements, with a configuration comparable in purpose to a commercial MicroTime 200 platform. Also supported instrument training.

Custom microscopy · Time-resolved fluorescence · System integration
PROJECT 02

Peltier-based microscope temperature controller

Designed a microscope temperature-control assembly in Autodesk Fusion 360, covering a 10–68 °C operating range. Work included instrument development, maintenance, and user training.

Thermal control · CAD · Microscope instrumentation
PROJECT 03

Optical tweezers integrated with STED microscopy

Worked with an Olympus IX-83 optical-tweezers setup and a commercial Abberior STED-ready system, integrating manipulation and super-resolution imaging capabilities for membrane research.

Optical trapping · STED · Instrument alignment
PROJECT 04

Tabletop optical tweezers with multiphoton imaging

Built and worked with a tabletop optical-tweezers microscope incorporating multiphoton imaging, combining optical manipulation and nonlinear fluorescence microscopy.

Optical tweezers · Multiphoton microscopy · Optical design
PROJECT 05

Mach–Zehnder interferometer

Developed an interferometric setup for ultrafast pulse characterization, applying precision alignment and interferometric measurement principles.

Interferometry · Ultrafast optics · Characterization
PROJECT 06

Femtosecond laser systems

Hands-on alignment, maintenance, and troubleshooting experience with a Coherent MIRA 900-F oscillator pumped by a Verdi 5, and a K&M Labs oscillator kit with Nd:YVO₄ / Verdi 5 pumping.

Femtosecond lasers · Laser alignment · Diagnostics
